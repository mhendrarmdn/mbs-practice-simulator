# v0.8 Strategy Lab

## Purpose

The Strategy Lab solves a problem with generic decision advice: a draft can be sensible for one objective and poor for another.

For example:

- a high-marketing, high-capacity plan can align with Market Leadership
- the same plan may conflict with Liquidity Defense
- a high-price, high-R&D plan may align better with Premium Brand

## Objectives

### Balanced Value
Balances profitability, liquidity, market position, capacity, and readiness.

### Profitability First
Weights margin, ROE, unit economics, and spending discipline most heavily.

### Market Leadership
Weights share, demand capture, marketing/R&D support, and supply readiness.

### Liquidity Defense
Weights cash resilience, current ratio, overdraft avoidance, and spending discipline.

### Capacity Growth
Weights demand gap, utilization, equipment investment, and supply readiness.

### Premium Brand
Weights quality, image, R&D, marketing, and pricing power.

## Important limitation

The alignment score is a transparent heuristic. It is not trained on official TopBoss winning data and is not a prediction of official NPV.
